FAQ

How do I subscribe?

Create an account, open Pricing, and Start Now. Stripe Checkout handles payment securely.

Can I upgrade or cancel?

Yes — from Dashboard → Billing → Customer Portal. No phone call required.

Is there Enterprise custom pricing?

No. AmberOne is self-service: Personal, Family, Executive, and Business plans only.

What about Family seats?

Family plans are available for 2, 5, or 10 users.
