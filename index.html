What AmberOne does

One brain. The list below is everything the website is built to handle — without burying you in price tags on every capability.

  • 1
    Talk to Amber Natural conversation to ask, command, plan, and finish work — not hunt menus.
  • 2
    Calendar & schedule Events, appointments, conflict checks, and day-level briefing from one place.
  • 3
    Reminders & follow-ups Never drop a due date, bill, chore, or next action.
  • 4
    Memory & notes Preferences, facts, and notes Amber can reuse so you don’t repeat yourself.
  • 5
    Daily briefing & evening recap Start oriented and close the day knowing what’s done and what’s next.
  • 6
    Family coordination Shared calendars, chores, school & sports, groceries, birthdays, and vacation planning.
  • 7
    Executive assist Meeting prep, email assistance, CRM health, travel, and professional follow-ups.
  • 8
    Business operations Appointments, customers, Instagram & Nextdoor picture ads on your calendar, reviews, and team scheduling.
  • 9
    Connections Wire calendar, email, and services so Amber works inside your real stack.
  • 10
    Guided setup Walk-throughs that stay until finished — explain, recommend, wait, verify, complete.
  • 11
    Billing & self-service Subscribe, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime through secure Stripe checkout and the Customer Portal.
  • 12
    Marketplace skills Optional packs for specialized workflows as Amber grows with your needs.
